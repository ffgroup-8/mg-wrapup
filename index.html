01 · The shift

From WordPress to built for what's next.

The old setup worked, but every site was its own maintenance job. Plugins piled up, content was duplicated, and each change meant touching many places. We replaced the foundation underneath every McNellie's website.

Where we started

WordPress Multisite + WooCommerce

  • Separate installs and plugin stacks to keep patched
  • Content copied by hand across group and restaurant sites
  • Menus kept in sync through a WordPress plugin
  • Inconsistent design from one concept to the next
  • Leftover boilerplate leaking into page titles and search results
  • Slow pages and limited control over SEO
→
Where you are now

Next.js + Sanity, headless

  • One content system for the group site and every restaurant
  • One restaurant platform, so a new brand is a profile, not a new build
  • Menus pulled live from Toast, reservations through OpenTable
  • Fast pages, and structured data on every location for Google and AI
  • Gift cards, merch and event tickets sold through headless Shopify

03 · The tech stack

Next.js, Sanity and Shopify, working as one.

Next.js builds the sites, Sanity runs the content and Shopify runs the commerce. All of it is shared: fix something once and it improves everywhere, and a new restaurant inherits all of it on day one.

One platform

Every restaurant, one engine

We didn't build 19 separate websites. One platform sees which restaurant's web address a guest typed in and shows that brand, with its own logo, fonts and colors. A fix or security update reaches every site at once.

Sanity

Edit without a developer

Your team builds and rearranges pages from 100+ ready-made sections, previews changes on the real site, and publishes in moments. Guardrails only offer sections that fit, so a page can't break.

Toast

Menus that update themselves

Menus come straight from your Toast point of sale. Change an item in Toast and the website follows, showing only what each brand actually serves.

Shopify

Gift cards, merch and tickets

A custom storefront with collections, sizes, cart and checkout. Physical and digital gift cards, event tickets and branded merch, all run from Shopify.

Bookings & rewards

The guest's next step, built in

OpenTable for reservations and private dining. Thanx for the rewards program and member tiers. Event and catering inquiries go straight to the right location's team, and the guest gets a confirmation.

Every guest

Built for every screen

Designed for phone, tablet and desktop, with smooth video, accessibility and privacy pages on every site, a cookie consent banner, and Google Analytics tracking every restaurant.

Built to stay currentModern, widely supported tools hosted on Vercel, with security patches applied as soon as they were released.
Cleaned up mid-projectA September 2026 overhaul made each brand a single profile, so every launch after it came together faster.
Documented for the futureStep-by-step playbooks for launching a new restaurant, redirects and Toast setup, so the platform can grow without us.

04 · The design system

One master library powers every brand.

Every color, logo, website and menu across the portfolio is built in Figma from a single master brand library. Each concept gets its own tokens, so Forno Santo still looks like Forno Santo, but everything underneath is shared. Change a style once and it carries through every file that uses it.

  1. Master libraryType, spacing, components and layout rules
  2. Concept tokensBrand, surface and text colors for each concept
  3. Logo systemsEvery lockup, badge, stamp and mark
  4. Websites & menusDesigned from the same parts, every time

06 · Search & AI

Found on Google. Named by AI.

Guests now ask ChatGPT and Google the same question. We built every page to win both: classic SEO underneath, with Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) on top so AI tools can read, trust and recommend your restaurants.

1AuditEvery page reviewed as built
2TitlesKeywords first, every page
3DescriptionsWritten to earn the click
4Social cardsClean previews when shared
5Body copySpecific dishes, places, landmarks
6Schema & FAQStructured data for every location

Rankings protected through the move. Every old WordPress address redirects to its new page, unfinished preview sites were hidden from Google, and every restaurant carries structured details (hours, locations, contact) that Google and AI assistants read directly.
